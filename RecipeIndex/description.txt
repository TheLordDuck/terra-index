Recipe Index is an NEI-style recipe browser for Terraria, plus a storage terminal that finds items across your chests.

RECIPE INDEX
Press N to open a searchable list of every item in the game, including items from other mods.
- Left click an item to see how it is crafted: ingredients, crafting stations and special conditions.
- Right click an item to see every recipe that uses it.
- Hover any item (inventory, chests, shops) and press [ for its recipes or ] for its uses.
- Click ingredients and stations inside a recipe to keep exploring; use Back to return.
- Ingredients turn green when you have enough in your inventory, red when you don't.
- Type @modname in the search box to filter by mod (for example @terraria or @calamity).

STORAGE TERMINAL
Buy the Terminal from the Mechanic for 5 silver and hang it on a background wall.
- Connect the terminal and your chests with the same red wire.
- Right click the terminal and start typing: the items in all connected chests are filtered as you type.
- Click an item and the chests holding it glow for 10 seconds. Chests off screen get an arrow on the edge of the screen showing the direction and distance.
- Right click an item in the terminal to open its recipe.

MULTIPLAYER
Fully supported. The server scans the chests and keeps the terminal updated, so you see changes made by other players.

All keys can be rebound in Settings > Controls. Available in English and Spanish.

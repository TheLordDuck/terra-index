[h1]Terra Index[/h1]
An NEI-style recipe browser for Terraria, a storage terminal that finds items across all your chests, ready-made buildings, wireless teleporters and a Calamity guide. Works in multiplayer and with other mods.

[h2]Recipe Index[/h2]
Press [b]N[/b] to open a searchable list of every item in the game, including items from other mods.
[list]
[*]Left click an item to see how it is crafted, right click to see every recipe that uses it.
[*]Hover any item (inventory, chests, shops) and press [b][[/b] for its recipes or [b]][/b] for its uses.
[*]Items that can't be crafted show which NPC sells them, which enemies drop them and which bags or crates contain them.
[*]Tabs by item type, filters by class, and [b]@modname[/b] in the search box to filter by mod.
[*]Crafting tree down to the base materials, minus what you already have.
[*]Set any item as your goal: an on-screen list shows what you are still missing, with a button that crafts the next step.
[*]Craft straight from your chests near a storage terminal, or anywhere with the Portable Terminal.
[*]Compare up to 3 weapons, tools, armor or accessories side by side.
[/list]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheLordDuck/terra-index/main/docs/screenshots/1%20-%20Recipe%20index.png[/img]

[h2]Storage Terminal[/h2]
Buy the Terminal from the Steampunker and hang it on a background wall. Connect it to your chests with red wire.
[list]
[*]Start typing and the items in all connected chests are filtered as you type.
[*]Click to take a stack out, or hold right click to pick an exact amount.
[*]Shift + click an item and the chests holding it glow, with arrows to the ones off screen.
[*]Deposit items, or your whole inventory at once, into the connected chests.
[*]Item tooltips say how many you have in your chests.
[*]After the Moon Lord, the Portable Terminal opens your terminal from anywhere.
[/list]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheLordDuck/terra-index/main/docs/screenshots/2%20-%20Index%20and%20terminal.png[/img]

[h2]Blueprints[/h2]
Not good at building? Craft a Blueprint (10 wood), pick a design and its wood, see a preview and click to build it, for free. Every room is valid NPC housing.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheLordDuck/terra-index/main/docs/screenshots/5%20-%20Placing%20a%20blueprint.png[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheLordDuck/terra-index/main/docs/blueprints/overview.png[/img]
The catalog's Town NPCs tab shows which biomes and neighbours each NPC loves and hates.

[h2]Calamity Guide[/h2]
Only with the Calamity Mod: the armor, weapons, accessories and potions the Calamity wiki recommends for your class (rogue included) at your point of the game. Craft the [b]Calamity Guide[/b] book (10 wood) to browse the build for every stage and see the bosses, events and minibosses you have beaten, the missing ones and the next ones.
Without Calamity none of this appears, and it can be turned off in the mod's settings (Calamity features).
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheLordDuck/terra-index/main/docs/screenshots/7%20-%20Calamity%20guide%20book.png[/img]

[h2]And more[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Wireless teleporters[/b]: put them on a channel with the Teleporter Linker and right click one to go to the next one.
[*][b]Target Dummy damage meter[/b]: total damage, DPS and last combo of each fight.
[*][b]Radio[/b]: a piece of furniture that plays clips at random moments, the same for every player.
[/list]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheLordDuck/terra-index/main/docs/screenshots/6%20-%20Teleporter%20channels.png[/img]

[h2]Multiplayer and languages[/h2]
Fully supported in multiplayer. Available in English, Spanish, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Italian, Polish and Simplified Chinese. Every key can be rebound in Settings > Controls.

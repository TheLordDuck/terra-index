# Terra Index

Terraria mod (tModLoader 1.4.4) by **thelordduck22** that ties recipes and storage together:

- **Recipe index**, NEI-style (from Minecraft): search any item, see how it is crafted, what it is used for and,
  if it can't be crafted, how to obtain it.
- **Crafting tree and goal**: the full tree of any recipe down to its base materials, minus what you already have.
  Set it as your goal and an on-screen list tracks what is still missing.
- **Item comparison**: up to 3 weapons, tools, armor or accessories side by side.
- **Storage terminal**: connect your chests with red wire, search them from the terminal, take items out of its
  door, deposit into the chests, and make the chest holding an item light up.
- **Crafting from your chests**: craft straight from the terminal's chests, with crafting stations near you or on
  its wire, or take out only the ingredients you are missing.
- **Portable terminal**: open your terminal from anywhere in the world.
- **Blueprints**: pick a ready-made house and its material, see where it goes, and build it with one click.
- **Both at once**: the recipe index opens next to the terminal, and delivery drones carry the items between the
  chests and the terminal.
- Settings for each player and for the server, 9 languages, and full multiplayer support.
